# T1305 - The viewer error card is only ever photographed at this box's 1.25 scale

## Summary

viewer-error-card.ps1 (T381) measures the painted card against viewer_error_card.layout at whatever DPI the box reports, which here is always 1.25. The unit lane asserts the ARITHMETIC at 1.0, 1.25, 1.5 and 2.0; the PAINT is proven at one of them. The interesting scales for a paint are the ones where a rounded rect's pen widens (scale >= 1.5 gives a 2px rim) and where the text lines are tallest, and neither has ever been on a screen. Needs a way to put the pane at another scale on the background test desktop - a per-monitor-DPI test desktop, or a window moved to a second display - which is the actual work here.
