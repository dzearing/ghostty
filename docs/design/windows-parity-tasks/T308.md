# T308 - The dialog panels (ActivityMonitor, MachineChooser, HeroCarousel rests) are still hardcoded dark

## Summary

T305 fixed the two surfaces T274 named — the caption band and the tab strip —
and took the invented accents out of every surface. What it deliberately did
NOT do is re-theme the PANELS, which are still written as dark-only: they open
dark on a light system theme, which is the panel-scale version of the same
defect.

`chrome_theme.zig` already answers this; nothing consumes it here yet.

## What is hardcoded at HEAD (VERIFIED by reading, 2026-08-01)

- `ActivityMonitor.zig:169-225` — about thirty `COLOR_*` constants
  (`COLOR_BG = RGB(32,32,32)`, `COLOR_TEXT = RGB(230,230,230)`,
  `COLOR_CARD_BG`, `COLOR_HEADER_BG`, the chart/gauge/dot palettes …). The
  accent is derived now; everything around it is not.
- `MachineChooser.zig:103` — `DIALOG_BG = RGB(32,32,32)`, and `ROW_BG` washed
  off it. `chooser_rows`' washes follow luminance since T305, so the module is
  ready; the two base constants are the whole remaining problem.
- `HeroCarousel.zig` — the two REST colors, `RGB(96,96,96)` (divider) and
  `RGB(110,110,110)` (tile border). Mid-greys, so they degrade rather than
  vanish on a light band, but they are still not derived from anything. (The
  divider's WIDTH and hover are T250's, separately.)

## Fix

Resolve each panel's base from `chrome_theme.chromeBase(window-theme,
background, systemUsesLightTheme())` and derive the rest of its ramp from the
palette, the way `Window.chromePalette()` now does. The semantic colors that
are NOT a wash of the surface — the status dots, the CPU/MEM series, the amber
warning — stay literal, but must be re-checked against their floors on a light
panel rather than assumed.

Watch the scale: `ActivityMonitor.zig` is a 3k-line file and T299 already wants
it split. Splitting the palette out is probably the first move, not a
thirty-site edit in place.

## Validation

- Extend `test/win32/chrome-theme.ps1`: open each panel under a light chrome
  base and assert the panel background is light and its primary text clears
  4.5:1 against it, computed from captured pixels.
- `activity-monitor.ps1`, `ipc-machine-chooser.ps1`, `hero-mode.ps1` stay
  ALL PASS. Both test lanes, `test-agent`, P1-P3.
