# T679 - tab strip rim is brighter than the banner card's now that the card is elliptical

## Summary

T124 made the banner/TOC card's specular rim a real elliptical gradient lit
from a point half a card-height above the card (Mac's `GlassCardBackground`).
The tab chiclet still reads `banner_card.RIM_TOP`/`RIM_BOT` as the endpoints
of a straight vertical ramp (`tab_shape.rimAlpha`), so its top edge is lit at
the full 0.28 while the card's brightest pixel is now ~0.18. Two pieces of
chrome that T206 deliberately gave the SAME rim are now visibly different.

## In plain terms

The little highlight along the top edge of a tab is brighter than the one
along the top edge of a banner card, even though they are supposed to look
like the same material. Nothing is broken — it just makes the tabs read as
slightly shinier than the card sitting a few inches below them.

## Details

`tab_shape.rimAlpha`'s own comment states the approximation and its premise:
"a tab is short enough that a linear ramp between the same two endpoints is
indistinguishable". That premise held while the card's ramp ALSO started at
`RIM_TOP`; it does not now, because `RIM_TOP` is a stop at the ellipse's
center, which sits above the card and is never reached by any pixel of it.

Two candidate fixes:

- Evaluate `banner_card.rimAlpha` against the TAB's own rect, so the tab is
  lit by the same overhead ellipse (cohesive by construction, the T206 rule).
  Costs a `sqrt` per rim pixel of the tab strip.
- Retune the tab's linear endpoints to the values the card's ellipse actually
  paints at its top and bottom edges. Cheaper, but it re-introduces exactly
  the hand-matched pair of numbers T206's shared constants exist to avoid.

Held out of T124 deliberately: it changes tab visuals, which is a different
surface with its own acceptance coverage (`test/win32/tab-strip*.ps1`).

## Validation criteria

- [ ] The tab's rim alpha at its top edge and the card's rim alpha at its top

- [ ] `tab_shape.rimAlpha`'s doc comment no longer claims an approximation it
      is not making.
- [ ] Tab-strip acceptance scripts stay ALL PASS.
