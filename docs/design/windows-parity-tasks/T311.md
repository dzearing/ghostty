# T311 - T227b: account row identity - avatar/monogram and Sign Out as a link (findings 5,6)

## Summary

Second of three splits of **T227**. `docs/design/win32-machine-chooser.md` §4
findings **5** and **6**, both on the chooser's account row
(`chooser_layout.zig:107-111`, `RelayAccountRow.zig`):

- **5 - no avatar and no monogram.** Windows draws a static text plus a
  150 DIP button. Mac (§2.4) draws the email and a link over a **34 px
  accent-gradient monogram circle**. The circle is the only identity cue on the
  surface and it is simply absent.
- **6 - "Sign Out" is a button where Mac has a link**, and the two captions
  ("Sign in with Google..." / "Sign Out") share one fixed 150 DIP slot, so the
  button is sized for the longer caption in both states - the same
  fixed-slot-vs-content defect T235 fixed on the tab strip.

Both are painting + layout on one row, which is why they are one task and not
part of T310's arithmetic pass.

## Details

- The monogram takes its fill from the resolved accent (`chrome_theme.accentOn`
  against the account row's surface) and its glyph from
  `color_math.contrastForeground` - never a hand-picked pair. The gradient is
  the Mac cue; on win32 a two-stop `GradientFill` or a flat accent are both
  acceptable, but state which and why in the task file.
- The account row's slot must size to its caption like the action row already
  does (`chooser_layout.actionRow` is the precedent: the caller measures, the
  module packs), so the Sign In and Sign Out states stop sharing one width.
- Consume T310's type ramp for the email (caption) and the link (body).

## Validation

- `chooser_layout` unit tests at 1.0/1.25/1.5/2.0: the monogram circle, the
  email/link stack and the right-aligned control all nest in the account band,
  and the slot width follows the measured caption.
- `test/win32/ipc-machine-chooser.ps1`: the monogram is present and its fill
  tracks a PINNED accent (T305's lesson - an oracle for a system-derived pixel
  must state its input and restore it in a `finally`).
- `-NegativeControl` fails a load-bearing pixel claim.
- Floor: both `zig build test` lanes, `test-agent`, P1-P3.
