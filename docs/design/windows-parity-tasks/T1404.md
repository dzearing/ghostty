# T1404 - a synchronous window capture can silently omit its child controls, and nothing in the harness says so

## Summary

`Get-TestWindowPixels -Sync` photographs a window through `PrintWindow`. What
the window paints itself is deterministic; a `WS_CHILD` control inside it is
not. T1400 measured eight captures of one unmoved viewer nav bar, at a constant
width, with the address field's rect reading a constant 122px from
`GetWindowRect`: the field's 35 full-height rows were in six of them and absent
from the other two. Both are valid bitmaps and the harness returns both without
comment, so the only symptom is an assertion downstream that flips verdicts on
identical code - which read for a day as *the working directory decides*.

T1400 wrote the rule into `docs/claude/testing.md` (measure a child through its
rect; bound an ink scan at its edge) and fixed the one script that was counting
a child's pixels. What does not exist is anything that CHECKS it. A probe that
starts measuring a child's pixels tomorrow gets the same day of investigation.

## In plain terms

When a test takes a picture of part of the app to check how it looks, the boxes
and text fields inside that part sometimes come out of the picture and sometimes
do not - and the test is handed the picture either way, with nothing to say a
piece is missing. One test was caught believing such a picture. Nothing stops
the next one.

## Details

Two shapes worth considering, in order:

1. **Make the omission LOUD.** A capture helper that knows the window's child
   controls could compare their rects against the bitmap and refuse (or report)
   a capture where a child's region is entirely the parent's background. That
   turns a silent wrong answer into a named one, which is the same move
   `Test-TestCaptureUniform` already makes for a blank capture.
2. **Make the capture deterministic**, if it can be: establish whether the
   omission is a `PrintWindow` child-enumeration race, a `WM_PRINT`
   `PRF_CHILDREN` behaviour of the system `Edit` class, or a DWM artifact, and
   whether a retry-until-children-appear is honest rather than a heuristic that
   hides a real product paint bug.

Note that `-AllowUniform` disables the one guard that exists here; a probe
carrying it has nothing left that notices a capture that drew less than it
should have.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] The mechanism behind the omission is named, from a measurement rather than
      from reasoning about the API.
- [ ] A probe that measures a child control's pixels through a parent capture is
      told so - by a refusal, a warning, or a helper that makes the correct
      thing the easy thing.
- [ ] The check is demonstrated to FAIL on the shape it exists to catch (a
      capture with the child's region absent), not only to pass on a good one.
- [ ] `test\win32\viewer-narrow-pane.ps1` and `test\win32\viewer-find.ps1` stay
      green.
