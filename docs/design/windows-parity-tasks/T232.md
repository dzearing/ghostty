# T232 - Tab strip spacing and glyph geometry are off

## Summary

User report with screenshot, 2026-07-31, verbatim:

> *"the plus icon has a huge left gap and no bottom gap ... we clearly need to
> make the tabs area a few px higher to both make room for a square plus icon
> plus consistent l/r/t/b gaps. And the plus icon itself, it looks like its left
> half of the horizontal line of the plus is shorter than the right half. wtf is
> wrong here. and the hamburger icon isn't wide enough by maybe 2px. and the x
> button's gap on the top touches the edge of the tab! AGAIN CONSISTENT MARGINS
> AND GAPS, ux shouldn't touch the edge of other ux!"*

Every item is arithmetic, not taste. **Rules and target numbers now live in
`docs/design/win32-design-system.md`** (written from this report); this task
brings the strip into compliance with it.

## Evidence (computed from the shipped constants, VERIFIED)

`tab_strip_layout.Metrics` gives `bar_h=32`, `tab_top_pad=3`, `btn_w=36`,
`close_btn_w=26`, `group_gap=8` DIP; `icon_button.Metrics` gives `target=26`
DIP. Resolving `targetBox` at the three common scales:

| Scale | Painted square | "+" gap above | "+" gap BELOW | "+" gap from tab (painted edge) | "x" gap above | "x" gap below |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.0 | 26 | 1 | 2 | **13** | 1 | 2 |
| **1.25** | 33 | 2 | **1** | **16** | **2** | 1 |
| 1.5 | 39 | 2 | 2 | **19** | 2 | 2 |

At the user's 125%: the "+" square sits **16 px** from the tab and **1 px** from
the strip's bottom edge. **A 16:1 ratio between two gaps that should be equal.**
Their "huge left gap and no bottom gap" is exact, and so is "the x button's gap
on the top touches the edge of the tab" (2 px at 125%, 1 px at 100%).

Two independent root causes, both named in the design system:

1. **Gaps were measured to the HIT BOX, not the painted edge.** The layout puts
   `group_gap` (8 DIP) between the tab and the "+" *button box*, and that box is
   36 DIP holding a 26 DIP square - so 5 DIP of invisible slack per side is
   added to a gap the user reads as one number. (Design system rule 2.)
2. **The band is too short for the control.** The button band is
   `tab_top_pad..bar_h` = 29 DIP holding a 26 DIP square: 1.5 DIP of slack,
   which truncates to 1-2 px and lands differently at each scale. Centering a
   control in a band too small for it does not pad it. (Design system rule:
   size the container to the control.)

For the glyphs, `paintIconButton` (`Window.zig:2700`) strokes with
`CreatePen` + `MoveToEx`/`LineTo`. `LineTo` **excludes the endpoint**, so a
stroke from `cx-h` to `cx+h` paints `cx-h .. cx+h-1` - asymmetric about the
center by one pixel - and a pen wider than 1 px biases its extra pixel to one
side. That is the "one arm shorter than the other" report, and it cannot be
fixed by nudging coordinates because the bias flips with DPI.

The hamburger is **INFERRED** but the user's optical read is almost certainly
right: `add` and `menu` share one mark extent (`h = glyph_px/3`), and a
horizontal-only glyph reads narrower than one with a vertical member at equal
geometric width.

## The fix

Per `win32-design-system.md`:

- **Icon button square 28 DIP**, hit box >= 32 DIP, `sm` (4 DIP) clear on all
  four sides of the painted square.
- **Tab band 36 DIP, `tab_top_pad` 4, `bar_h` 40 DIP** - which is also
  Windows Terminal's measured strip height, so this moves toward the platform
  reference rather than away from it. This is the "make the tabs area a few px
  higher" the user asked for, and it is what makes the 4 DIP gaps fit.
  **Interaction with T234:** if T234 lands first the strip is hidden entirely
  at one tab, so this height applies only with 2+ tabs. The two do not
  conflict; do T232 first (it is the smaller change).
- **Gaps computed between painted edges.** `group_gap` separates the tab's
  painted right edge from the button's painted square; the hit box then expands
  symmetrically around the square and is allowed to overlap the gap.
- **Glyphs become filled rects** (axis-aligned: `add`, `menu`) and filled
  polygons (`close`, chevrons). Symmetry asserted, not intended.
- **Optical mark widths per glyph**: `add` 12, `close` 11, `menu` **14** DIP.
  This replaces the single shared `h`, so the existing "close, add and menu
  share one optical width" test must be rewritten to assert the optical
  relationship rather than raw equality.

## Blast radius (do not miss this)

`bar_h` is the DPI oracle for three acceptance scripts, which compute
`scale = barH / 32.0`:

- `test/win32/tab-strip.ps1:128`
- `test/win32/tab-color.ps1:195`
- `test/win32/title-font.ps1:122`

All three must move to `/ 40.0` **in the same commit**, or every geometry
assertion in them silently rescales and starts passing against wrong pixels.

## Validation

- Unit tests in `tab_strip_layout.zig` / `icon_button.zig`, at 1.0, 1.25, 1.5,
  2.0: every painted edge >= 4 DIP from its container's edges and neighbours;
  the tab->"+" painted gap equals `group_gap` exactly; every glyph satisfies
  `min + max == 2 * center` on both axes.
- `test/win32/tab-strip.ps1` ALL PASS x3 with its negative control still
  failing, after the `/40.0` update.
- On-box screenshot at 125% compared against the user's: no gap ratio worse
  than 1:1.5 anywhere in the strip.

## What landed

**Geometry, all of it in the pure modules.**

- `icon_button.Metrics`: painted square **26 -> 28 DIP**, plus a new `hit_pad`
  (2 DIP) so the hit box is the square grown symmetrically to **32 DIP**.
  `hitBox()` is the exact inverse of `targetBox()` (asserted at six scales x
  twenty positions), which is what lets the strip lay out PAINTED edges while
  every existing call site keeps hit-testing the box it always had.
- `tab_strip_layout.Metrics`: **`bar_h` is now derived, not chosen** —
  `tab_top_pad + pad_sm + icon_button.target + pad_sm` = 4 + 4 + 28 + 4 = **40
  DIP**. `tab_top_pad` 3 -> 4, `text_pad` 10 -> 8 (both were off the 4 DIP
  scale), `close_btn_w` 26 -> 32 (the same square as its two neighbours),
  `btn_w` 36 -> 32. New `pad_sm` so every 4 DIP gap is computed from ONE
  constant and they cannot round apart at fractional DPI.
- `layout()` now computes in painted edges throughout and derives hit boxes at
  the end (`Metrics.buttonHit`). `closeRect` places the painted square
  `pad_sm` inside the tab's right edge instead of placing a box and letting the
  paint fall where it fell.
- `Window.tabBarHeight()` read `32.0 * scale` from a second copy of the
  constant; it now asks the layout module.

**Glyphs.** `glyphStrokes` -> `glyphQuads`: filled quads via `Polygon` +
`NULL_PEN` (new `src/apprt/win32/icon_button_paint.zig`, shared by the strip
and the banner chevron so neither can invent its own), replacing `CreatePen` +
`MoveToEx`/`LineTo`. Per-glyph optical extents (`close` 11, `add` 12, `menu`
14 DIP) replace one shared `h`, and every extent is **parity-matched to the
square's side** so `(side - extent) / 2` is an exact integer — that is what
makes symmetry constructive rather than lucky.

**A bug the user caught mid-task**, and the reason §4.3 now exists in the
design system: the first cut of the "x" set its corner offset `k` to `1.5 *
stroke_w`, reading the 45° relation backwards. A diagonal band's two long
edges are `2k` apart vertically, so its perpendicular thickness is `k * sqrt2`,
not `k / sqrt2` — 2x too heavy, which painted a **filled bowtie** with only its
four tips showing. Verbatim: *"what is wrong with the x icon on the tab??? it
should be the standard X close icon, not some weird variant"*. Fixed to
`k ~= 3t/4`, and pinned by a test asserting `|k*sqrt2 - stroke_w| <= 1` at
every scale from 1.0 to 3.0.
