# T299 - ActivityMonitor.zig is a mega file (3k lines): split the painter and the source/connection plane out

## Summary

`src/apprt/win32/ActivityMonitor.zig` is **~3,000 lines / 110 KB** after T296,
against a standing bar that says *"No mega files. Split modules as they grow"*
(CLAUDE.md, the user's 2026-07-12 quality bar). It grew by accretion across
T285 (the window), T286 (process control), T295 (remote sources) and T296 (the
carousel), and each of those increments was individually reasonable.

The pure arithmetic was split out at every step and is fine —
`activity_layout.zig`, `activity_rows.zig`, `activity_actions.zig`,
`activity_cards.zig`, `trend_gauge.zig`. What is left in the big file is three
separable win32 concerns wearing one hat.

## The seams (already visible as section banners in the file)

1. **The painter** — `paint`, `paintCarousel`/`paintCard`, `paintGauges`,
   `paintControlBar`, `paintTable`, `paintRow`, `paintEmptyState`,
   `paintBanner`, `paintScrollThumb`, plus the palette and the GDI helpers
   (`fill`, `drawText`, `roundRect`, `ellipse`). ~900 lines that take a `Layout`
   and an HDC and touch no lifetime.
2. **The source / connection plane** — `startDial`, `dialWorker`, `onDialed`,
   `adoptDial`, `beginMetrics`, `onMetrics`, `startMachineList`,
   `machineListWorker`, `onMachines`, `switchToCard`, `teardownSource`,
   `resetForNewSource`, plus `RemoteConn` / `DialResult` / `MachineListResult`.
   This is the part with the ownership rules, and the part where a mistake
   leaks a connection or frees a live window's shell.
3. **The window itself** — creation, the registry, the wndProc, input routing,
   the sampling worker.

## Why it matters here specifically

Group 2 is where every dangerous rule lives: unsubscribe before join, join
before free, a borrowed connection is never freed, bump the serial so a stale
dial frees itself, drop a sample by generation. Rules like that are auditable
when they sit in one small file with their own tests, and invisible when they
are spread through a 3,000-line one.

## Details

Prior art is the sibling split this file's own header cites —
`chooser_layout.zig` / `MachineChooser.zig` — and
`IpcServer` / `IpcHandlers` / `IpcRegistry`. The painter can take an explicit
context (fonts, scale, palette) rather than `*ActivityMonitor`, which also makes
parts of it testable.
