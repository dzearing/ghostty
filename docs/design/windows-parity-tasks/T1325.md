# T1325 - The vendored asset mirror is stale against origin/main and nothing reopens the check when main moves

## Summary

Found while landing T1321. `test\win32\hook-json.ps1` section A asserts that
`src\apprt\win32\assets\ghoztty\upstream\` is a byte-for-byte mirror of
tip-of-main's `macos/Resources/Ghoztty/`. Two of those assertions are RED right
now, and were red before this turn touched anything:

```
FAIL upstream mirror of skills/ghoztty/SKILL.md is byte-identical to origin/main
FAIL upstream mirror of skills/process-feedback/SKILL.md is byte-identical to origin/main
```

`origin/main` has moved on (`fc7e36356 feat(viewer): native image panes, and an
always-pinned nav bar` and friends touch `macos/Resources/Ghoztty/skills/`);
our mirror still reflects an older main, and the live `skills/ghoztty/SKILL.md`
tracks the mirror, so the skill document the Windows app EMBEDS is missing
whatever main has since said.

The failure mode is the interesting half. `scripts\guard-due.ps1` reopens a
harness when the files it COVERS change - all of them in our tree. Nothing in
our tree changes when `origin/main` advances, so this check goes red silently
and stays that way until an unrelated edit happens to reopen the guard. That is
the T1099 shape (a verdict that can only ever say "fine" until something else
pokes it), and it is why nobody noticed.

Two halves to fix:

1. Re-vendor the mirror from `origin/main` and bring the live unforked copy
   with it, taking whatever main has said about the `ghoztty` skill since. The
   three deliberate forks (`ghoztty-banner.sh`, `ghoztty-activity-state.sh`,
   `skills/process-feedback/SKILL.md`) must survive the re-vendor - hook-json's
   fork assertions are what prove that.
2. Make main's advance REOPEN the question, so this cannot go silently red
   again: the `hook-json` guard row (or a sibling) needs to key on the
   `origin/main` sha as well as on our covered files, the same way the main
   intake watermark does.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] `test\win32\hook-json.ps1` is ALL PASS with the mirror re-vendored.
- [ ] The three deliberate forks still carry their divergence markers after the
      re-vendor.
- [ ] Advancing `origin/main` past the vendored sha reopens the guard (proved
      against a synthetic sha, not by waiting for main).
