# T1319 - The watchdog decides by transcript age, so it never nudged a 2.5-hour stall

## In plain terms

The loop stopped working just before midnight and sat there for two and a half
hours. The thing whose whole job is to notice that and poke it kept writing
"healthy" into its log every five minutes, because *something* had touched the
window recently - a half-typed command that was never sent.

## What happened (2026-09-04)

| | |
|---|---|
| 23:41 | Turn 86 finishes T1311, reports what it did, stops |
| ~00:10 | `/rc` appears in the composer - **typed, never submitted** |
| 00:19-00:41 | Watchdog logs `healthy: ... age=11.41m → 31.51m (by=transcript)` |
| 00:43 | Health line: `DOWN ... turn=86 turn_age=2h 32m state=stale-heartbeat` |

Two clocks again, and this time they disagreed in the worst direction: the
**observer** was right and the **actor** was wrong.

- `go-loop-health.ps1` reported `turn_age=2h 32m` and `DOWN`. That is T1290's
  fix working exactly as intended.
- `go-loop-watchdog.ps1` computed `age=31.51m(by=transcript)` - measuring the
  keystrokes that typed `/rc` at ~00:10, not any work - stayed under its
  45-minute threshold, and did nothing.

So T1290 converted the reporting and left the deciding behind. The health line
told the truth to a supervisor session that only looks every two hours; the
watchdog, which looks every five minutes and is the only thing that recovers a
loop unattended, went on believing the transcript.

## The other half: the turn ended without submitting

The turn itself failed go.md step 7 - it ended by reporting success with `/rc`
half-typed in the composer instead of submitting the continuation. That is the
failure mode the start-go skill names ("a turn that stops to report success
kills the loop silently"), and it is worth fixing on its own. But note the
interaction: **a half-typed command is the perfect camouflage**, because typing
it refreshes exactly the signal the watchdog trusts. The two defects conceal
each other, which is why this went 2.5 hours rather than 45 minutes.

## What to build

- **Give the watchdog the same clock as the health line.** Its re-entry
  decision should key off the lock's turn counter and heartbeat - work
  completed - with transcript age at most a secondary signal. `turn_age`
  already exists; the watchdog should read it.
- **Treat a non-empty composer as suspicious, not as activity.** A pane with
  unsent text and no turn progress is a stalled turn, not a working one.
- **Keep the failure visible.** The watchdog's log said `healthy` 30+ times
  during a dead loop; whatever it decides on, its log line should show the
  number it decided on so a wrong call is readable afterwards.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] A loop whose turn counter has not advanced past the threshold is nudged,
      regardless of transcript activity.
- [ ] The 2026-09-04 shape is reproduced in a test - fresh transcript, unsent
      composer text, turn unchanged for hours - and the watchdog acts on it.
- [ ] The watchdog log names the age it decided on and which clock it came
      from, so `healthy` can be audited.
- [ ] go.md step 7's submission is verified rather than assumed - a turn that
      types the continuation without sending it is caught.
