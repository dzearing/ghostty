# T543 - Viewer TOC card polish: compact overlay slide animation and translucent pinned header

## In plain terms

The new table-of-contents card in narrow viewer panes appears and disappears
instantly when you toggle it, where the Mac version slides in from the left
edge with a short fade. And the pinned "CONTENTS" header at the top of the
card is a solid strip, where the Mac's is translucent glass the rows blur
through as they scroll under it. Both are refinements — everything works —
but they are the difference between "native card" and "the same card as the
Mac's".

## Summary

T160 shipped the win32 TOC card with two deliberate simplifications:

1. **No slide animation.** Mac parks the compact card off the left edge and
   animates a Core Animation transform (0.26s move + fade) on toggle
   (`ViewerView.applySidePanelState`). The win32 panel is a plain
   ShowWindow/SW_HIDE. A timer-driven position animation
   (`SetWindowPos` per frame, ~150ms ease-out) would match; `AnimateWindow`
   with `AW_HOR_POSITIVE | AW_SLIDE` is the cheaper native option worth
   evaluating first.
2. **Opaque header.** Mac's `SidePanelHeader` sits on `glassBackdrop()` so a
   row scrolling beneath stays a recognizable shape. The win32 header is an
   opaque band of the card fill. GDI has no live blur; an approximation is
   compositing the rows into the header band at reduced alpha (the
   BannerOverlay collapse-fade pattern), which reads as translucency without
   a blur pass.
