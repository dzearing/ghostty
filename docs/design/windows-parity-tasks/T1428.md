# T1428 - A machine whose re-enrollment failed while offline does not come back until the app is restarted

## Summary

Follow-up to T1425. The suspended-enrollment restore runs at sign-in and at every launch, so a user who signs back in while the relay is unreachable gets their own machine back only after quitting and reopening Ghoztty - which nobody thinks to do. The pending REVOCATION beside it already has a capped backoff for exactly this reason (relay_revoke_pending.backoffMs) and the restore has none; the Mac seat retries on a network-came-back transition. Give relay_suspend the same cadence, and prove it the way section 9 does: a relay that is DEAD when the restore first runs and answers afterwards.
