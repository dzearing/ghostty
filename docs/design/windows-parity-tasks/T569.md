# T569 - Shift+Tab through the Activity Monitor panel is untested on the box

## Summary

handleKey's backwards Tab walk reads GetKeyState(VK_SHIFT), and Shift+Tab is the ONE-step route from the filter field to the machine carousel (forward Tab takes four). Nothing on the box asserts it. The harness posts WM_KEYDOWN and fakes modifier state with SetKeyboardState, which is exactly the mechanism this repo has already recorded as unreliable for GetKeyState reads across processes - so the backwards half of the cycle may be broken in the product, broken in the harness, or fine, and today no evidence distinguishes those. Establish which, then either assert the walk in test/win32/activity-monitor.ps1 section L or record the mechanism limit the way TestDesktop.ps1 records its others.
