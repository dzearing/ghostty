# T1426 - The chooser never says a machine is still connected to an account you signed out of

## In plain terms

If you sign out anyway after Ghoztty could not remove this machine from your
account, you are told it stays connected — and then nothing ever tells you
whether it still is. The machine list is the one place that could say so, and it
says nothing. You are left to assume the sign-out you were warned about somehow
completed.

## Goals

- [ ] The chooser marks a machine that is still connected to an account it was signed out of
- [ ] The mark says what to do about it, not just that something is wrong
- [ ] It clears the moment the pending revocation completes (T1424)
- [ ] It reads correctly in both themes and at the chooser's narrow widths

## Summary

Split out of T1421; the Mac counterpart is the last bullet of `f3b1e5fb5`
("The chooser now says when a machine is still connected to the account it was
signed out of, instead of leaving the user to assume the sign-out they were
warned about completed").

Depends on T1424 because the pending-revocation record is what the chooser would
read to know the state exists at all.

Windows pieces: `src/apprt/win32/MachineChooser.zig` (the device rows and the
footer hint), `src/apprt/win32/RelayAccountRow.zig` (the pure text — the
sign-out sentences from T1421 live there and the new one belongs beside them).

## Validation criteria

- [ ] A unit test of the pure row/hint text for the still-connected state
- [ ] A pixel/DOM check that the mark renders in both themes at the chooser's narrow width
- [ ] Arming a pending revocation shows the mark; clearing it removes the mark
- [ ] `scripts\floor-lane.ps1 -Lane all` green
