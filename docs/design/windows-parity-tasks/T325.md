# T325 - contrastAdjustedTo lands under its own floor after 8-bit quantization (measured 2.9905 vs 3.0)

Found 2026-08-01 while writing T318's badge palette, by asserting the floor the
function promises and watching it fail.

## The defect (MEASURED, not inferred)

`color_math.contrastAdjustedTo(base, bg, target)` binary-searches CIELAB
lightness for the smallest move that clears `target`, evaluating each probe
through `ratioAgainst` - which works off `Lab.toSrgb()`, deliberately *"so gamut
clamping is seen but 8-bit quantization is not"* (`color_math.zig:1425-1430`).
The value it RETURNS, though, is `lab.toRgb()` - quantized to 8 bits. So the
color handed back can sit just under the floor the search just cleared.

Measured on the T318 session-card surface (`cardFill` of the two Fluent
surfaces), via a throwaway `zig test` that printed the ratios:

| surface | tone | ratio |
|---|---|---|
| `#202020` (dark) | warn / danger / good | 6.53 / 4.05 / 6.47 |
| `#F3F3F3` (light) | warn | **3.0003** |
| `#F3F3F3` (light) | danger | **2.9937** |
| `#F3F3F3` (light) | good | **2.9905** |

Three of six land within 0.01 of 3.0, and two land UNDER it. The light surface
is where it bites because the search has to travel farther there.

## Why it is not just a test-tolerance question

`chrome_theme`'s own palette tests already carry the workaround -
`>= ui_contrast_target - 0.05` at `chrome_theme.zig:295/:336/:337` - and T318
had to adopt the same tolerance (`chooser_sessions.zig`, the badge/dot contrast
test). That is three test sites agreeing to accept a value the design system
says is the floor. The floor is stated as WCAG 1.4.11's 3:1 in
`docs/design/win32-design-system.md`; a 2.99 accent is not a visible defect, but
"the function that enforces the floor does not quite enforce it" is exactly the
kind of quiet drift the contrast work exists to prevent, and every `accentOn`
caller inherits it (chooser row pill, Activity Monitor cards, tab strip accent,
danger buttons, T318's badges).

## The fix (proposed)

Evaluate the search's ACCEPTANCE test on the quantized color - i.e. check
`wcagContrastRatio(wcagLuminance(lab.toRgb()), bg_lum)` for the candidate that
will actually be returned, keeping the float path only for the reachability
probe at the endpoint. Then drop the `- 0.05` tolerance from all three test
sites, which is what proves the fix.

Watch for: the search must stay monotonic under quantization (a probe that
rounds the wrong way must not stall the bisection), and `contrastForeground`'s
black/white fallback must stay reachable.

## In plain terms

The design system promises that every colored mark in the chrome - a status
dot, a badge, the accent pill, the red close glyph - stands off its background
by a stated amount, so it stays visible for someone with low vision or on a
washed-out screen. The helper that enforces that promise measured the color it
was considering, then handed back a slightly different one, and the difference
was occasionally enough to put the mark just under the line. Nobody could see
it; what they could see was five different tests quietly agreeing to accept
"close enough" instead of the number the design system states. After this, the
promise and the measurement are about the same color.
