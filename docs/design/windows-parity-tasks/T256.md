# T256 - Repair tab-strip.ps1 and menu-bar.ps1 for the caption band

## Summary

**Split out of T254 mid-flight, and it is the reason T254 is not `done`.**

T254 gave the caption band to the client area, so the tab strip now begins at
client `y = captionHeight()` (36 DIP) instead of `y = 0`. In the app that is
one number, applied in one place (`Window.tabBarTop()`, consumed by the strip's
BitBlt destination and by `inTabBar`/`toStripY` on the way in). In the
ACCEPTANCE SCRIPTS it is not: `tab-strip.ps1` and `menu-bar.ps1` both derive
the strip's band from the client rect's own top, and both fail against a
correct build.

Measured on 2026-07-31, immediately after T254's implementation was green on
its own script and on all three zig lanes and P1-P3:

```
tab-strip: 7 FAILED / 28 passed
menu-bar: 19 FAILED / 25 passed
```

This is exactly the blast radius **T232** wrote down — *"`bar_h` is the DPI
oracle for three acceptance scripts"* — arriving one task later than expected,
because T254 changed the strip's ORIGIN rather than its height.

## The fix

Both scripts already compute the strip band from measured geometry rather than
constants; they just measure it from the wrong datum. Give them the caption
offset the same way the app gets it — derived, not hard-coded:

- The strip's top is `client.top + captionHeight`, and `captionHeight` is
  `round(36 * scale)` where `scale` comes from the DPI they already read
  (`Get-TestWindowDpi`). Better still, take it the way `tab-strip.ps1` takes
  the bar height today: from the pane child's rect vs the client rect, which
  is self-relative and holds at any DPI. The pane top minus the strip height
  IS the caption height, with no constant at all.
- Recheck every scanline y, every posted click y, and the menu-popup anchor
  expectations in `menu-bar.ps1`.

Do NOT paper over it by giving the scripts a fixed 36: the whole point of the
self-relative idiom is that the number can change (T205 will change it again
when the strip moves INTO the caption row).

## Why this is its own task

T254's context was exhausted by the time this surfaced (the go.md ~250k rule),
and the repair is script work with its own verification loop, not more app
code. T254's implementation is committed and green on its own terms — three
zig lanes, `test/win32/caption-bar.ps1` ALL PASS with a failing negative
control, and P1-P3 ALL PASS. What is red is these two scripts' expectations.

**Until this lands the standing floor is NOT green**, so this is the next task
regardless of what else is queued.

## What it actually took

The caption offset was the easy half, and it went in as written above.

- **`tab-strip.ps1`** — `$scale` no longer comes from `barH / 40.0` (which was
  reading caption + strip and reported **2.375** at a real 1.25, skewing every
  DIP constant in the script). It comes from `Get-TestWindowDpi`, the same
  `GetDpiForWindow` the app derives `Window.scale` from. `$capH` is then
  `caption_layout`'s own construction at that scale, `$barH` is the REMAINDER up
  to the pane child's top, and a new `$stripTop` replaces `$offY` in every
  scanline. The old "the bar is visible" positive control became `barH ==
  3*sm + sq` — measuring the strip and checking it against its own construction
  catches a wrong `$capH` as a wrong `$barH`, rather than letting it skew
  silently.
- **`menu-bar.ps1`** — one `Caption-Height` helper, applied in the two places
  that convert a strip y into a screen y: `Click-Strip` (which now takes a
  STRIP-relative y, which is what all eleven call sites were already passing)
  and the `Ink` sampler's origin.

That took menu-bar from 19 failures to 4 — and the remaining 4 were **not the
caption band at all**. `Strip-Geometry` re-implemented `tab_strip_layout`'s tab
SIZING to find the "+", and T235 had changed that rule out from under it one
task earlier: modelled width ~250 px (equal share, capped at the retired 200
DIP) against a real content-sized ~344 px, so the "+" click landed back inside
tab 1 and no tab was ever created. Four assertions downstream of "a second tab
exists" fell with it.

**A script cannot re-derive a width that comes from text metrics, and it should
not try** — that is T249's point arriving early. So the tab run is no longer
modelled: only the two right-anchored buttons are derived (`padR` + the shared
28 DIP square, which is anchoring, not sizing), and the last tab's painted right
edge is MEASURED off a capture — scanning leftward from just inside the menu
button along a row near the strip's bottom, the first non-strip-background pixel
is the tab. That row is below the "+" glyph's extent, so nothing between the two
can be mistaken for it. `Start-Gui` gained `--background=#000000` to make that
scan safe: an inactive tab's fill is the strip background lifted by
`tab_shape.INACTIVE_LIFT` (6% of white), which is ~14 levels on a dark strip and
~1 level on a light one.

Also corrected while in there: `Strip-Geometry`'s `$btnW` was 36 DIP, the
pre-T232 hit box. Gaps are measured against the 28 DIP PAINTED square.
