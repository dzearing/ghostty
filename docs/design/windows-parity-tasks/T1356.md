# T1356 - The app crashes when a window holding a viewer pane is closed

## Summary

Closing a window that owns a viewer pane takes the whole app down with a use-after-free. The WebView2 controller's Close() pumps messages from inside ViewerPane.deinit, the pane's own wndProc runs during that pump and calls Window.updateDimOverlays(), and the sweep walks the surface the deinit is in the middle of freeing.

## In plain terms

Close a window that has a document/diff viewer open in it and Ghoztty
disappears — every window, not just that one. Anything unsaved in the other
panes goes with it. The crash is reliable enough that an acceptance script had
to stop closing such a window to avoid tripping over it.

## Where

- `src/apprt/win32/ViewerPane.zig:765` (`deinit` → the WebView2 `close()` that
  pumps messages) and `ViewerPane.zig:5724` (the `wndProc` arm that calls
  `Window.updateDimOverlays()` during that pump).
- `src/apprt/win32/Window.zig:3253` (`updateDimOverlays`) and
  `src/apprt/win32/PaneView.zig:190` (`showDimOverlay`, where the freed
  surface is dereferenced).

## Evidence

Reproduced on 2026-09-05 while validating T543, from
`test\win32\viewer-narrow-pane.ps1`: `+close` on a window holding a viewer.

```
Segmentation fault at address 0x1ab75702350
  PaneView.zig:190  showDimOverlay
  Window.zig:3253   updateDimOverlays
  ViewerPane.zig:5724 wndProc          <- re-entered from the pump below
  webview2_iface.zig:1423 close
  ViewerPane.zig:765 deinit
```

WER: `ghoztty.exe 0xc000041d STATUS_FATAL_USER_CALLBACK_EXCEPTION`.
