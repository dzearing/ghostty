# T1445 - after dismissing the update notice there is nowhere to see that the app is out of date

## Summary

Found while building T626. When this app is the older side of a protocol skew
it now raises a tray notice — once per run, saying "update Ghoztty" — and that
is the whole of the surface. A balloon times out in ten seconds. A user who
looks away, or who clicks it, checks for an update and puts it off, has no
standing place to see that their terminal is running without session
persistence and why.

## In plain terms

Ghoztty tells you once that it is out of date and that your sessions are not
being kept. Miss that notice and there is nothing on screen that still says so
— the About window does not mention it, and neither does anything else. It
should be findable after the fact, not only at the moment it happens.

## Goals

- [ ] A persistent surface states the condition while it lasts (About is the
      obvious candidate; a window-level indicator is another).
- [ ] It clears itself when the skew is gone, without needing a restart to
      look right.
- [ ] The once-per-run balloon stays as it is — this is the place to look it
      up, not a second nag.

## Details

`App.notifyAppOlderThanAgent` raises the notice; the condition itself is
`agent_upgrade.evaluateSkew(...) == .skew_app_older`, re-evaluated on the two
`refreshLocalAgentIfStale` triggers. Whatever surface this lands on needs the
live answer rather than the once-per-run flag (`agent_skew_notice_shown`),
which is a "have we said it" marker and not a state.

Priority P3 deliberately: the user IS told, once, with the act named — this is
the durable place to look it up afterwards.

## Validation criteria

- [ ] An arm in `test/win32/agent-upgrade.ps1` (or the About harness) that
      finds the condition stated on the persistent surface while the skew is
      live, with the same `GHOZTTY_AGENT_PROTO_VERSION` hook arm K uses.
- [ ] A negative control: no skew, nothing stated.
- [ ] Both zig test lanes, `test-agent`, and P1-P3 stay green.
