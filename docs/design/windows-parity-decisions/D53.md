# D53 - Should the win32 banner collapse cross-fade the body, or is the clipped reveal enough?

## Why this needs a call

Mac's animation resizes the card AND cross-fades the body, and SwiftUI gives it the second half for free. GDI does not: text has no opacity, so a cross-fade means rendering the content into an offscreen DIB and AlphaBlending it, which also means untangling the link hit rects that the content pass builds on the way through. The resize was the part that was actually missing - the collapse read as a jump cut - so I shipped that and left the fade as a named follow-up rather than growing the task.
