# D53 - When a banner collapses on Windows, should its text fade away as the card shrinks (like Mac), or slide out of view under the shrinking edge?

## The experience gap, in plain terms

When you dismiss a banner above a pane, the card animates closed. On the Mac, two things happen at once: the card shrinks, and the text inside it fades to transparent as it goes - the content reads as "going away". On Windows today, the card shrinks with the same motion and timing as the Mac, but the text keeps full strength and is progressively covered by the shrinking edge (softened by the existing bottom fade) - the content reads as "being covered". The whole difference lasts about a fifth of a second.

This is one to judge by eye: collapse a banner on both machines, or just on Windows, and decide whether the Windows version reads as wrong or cheap. If it looks fine, this stays as shipped and the follow-up task is dropped; if it reads as a downgrade, T677 adds the true fade.

## The technical background (skippable)

Mac gets the fade for free from SwiftUI. On Windows, GDI text has no opacity, so a true cross-fade means rendering the banner content into an offscreen bitmap and alpha-blending it every frame, plus untangling the link hit-rectangles that the content pass builds on the way through. The resize was the part whose absence read as a jump cut, so that shipped first with the fade filed as T677.

## Resolution

Chosen: Text slides out under the shrinking edge - as shipped, fade stays a filed follow-up (Recommended)
