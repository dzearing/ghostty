# D81 - Should the title/tab band be see-through like Windows Terminal, or stay tinted to your terminal's own color?

## Why this needs a call

Windows 11's signature window look is Mica: a faint, see-through material that tints the top of a window with the desktop wallpaper behind it. Windows Terminal uses it, and it is a big part of why its tab strip reads as part of the window frame rather than a bar sitting on top. Ghoztty on Windows does the opposite today - the title and tab band is tinted from YOUR TERMINAL's background color, which is what the Mac build does and what lets the selected tab blend straight into the text area below it. Both are defensible; they just cannot both be true, because one takes its color from your desktop and the other from your terminal. Measured on the box before filing: with the material switched on, the band no longer tracks the terminal, and in the only configuration where it actually appeared the title text fell to 3.0:1 contrast against it.

## Resolution

Chosen: Keep the band tinted to your terminal (Recommended)
