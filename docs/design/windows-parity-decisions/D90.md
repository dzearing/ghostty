# D90 - The D: drive's connection has been logging ~267 corrected hardware errors a day - reseat it, slow it down, or watch it?

## Why this needs a call

This is a report about your hardware, not about Ghoztty - nothing in the terminal can act on it, which is why it comes to you. Over the last 30 days Windows logged 8020 corrected hardware errors, all from one internal connection: the PCIe link to the Samsung 990 PRO that is your D: drive - the disk holding this repo, the build cache and the work. Windows caught and corrected every one, so nothing has been lost and this is not what has been crashing the tests. But the rate has risen from about 200 a day in early August to about 267 a day now, and a link that retries that often is marginal rather than healthy. What you cannot tell from here is whether it is the drive, the slot, or the seating - that is a physical call, and the three options differ in what you would actually do about it this afternoon.
