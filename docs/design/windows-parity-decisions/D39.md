# D39 - When the Activity Monitor is minimised, should its per-machine probes drop their connections or just pause the readings?

## Why this needs a call

The panel is a window people leave open for hours, and most of those hours it is minimised or on another virtual desktop. T290 already decided that a panel nobody can see must not enumerate processes; the same reasoning applies to holding one network connection per machine open. But there are two ways to honour it, and they differ in what the user sees on the way back.
