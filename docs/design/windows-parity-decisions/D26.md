# D26 - Across a sampling suspension, should the Activity Monitor's trend charts clear or carry a gap marker?

## Why this needs a call

The panel now stops sampling while nobody can see it (T290), which creates a stretch of time with no data. The trend rings are indexed by sample rather than by time, so simply resuming would paint however long the panel was away as a single pixel - the chart would misrepresent its own X axis. Something has to give: either the history or the ring's simple shape.

## Resolution

Chosen: Clear the rings on resume (Recommended)
