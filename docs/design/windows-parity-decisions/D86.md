# D86 - When a viewer pane gets too narrow for its address bar, should the address box shrink or disappear?

## Why this needs a call

Squeeze a viewer pane down and its little toolbar runs out of room. The buttons now fold into a '...' menu, which you approved - but the address box has no equally obvious answer, and you said so: 'i am quite unsure how an address bar is going to look in a tiny condensed mode. 1 button and 1 tiny input?'. On the box, a pane below about 145 px has room for the menu button and roughly a hundred pixels of box; below that something has to give. What you would see differs by option: either the box vanishes cleanly at a threshold, or it keeps shrinking to a few characters, or it turns into another button.

## Resolution

Chosen: Legible or gone - never a stub (Recommended)
